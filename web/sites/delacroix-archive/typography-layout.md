# Delacroix Archive typography and layout

- Chinese headings and text use an available Song/serif stack with conservative
  fallbacks; UI metadata uses the sans stack.
- English and French display headings may use italic serif styling; controls,
  dates and evidence locators remain upright and explicit.
- The hero name is two intentional full lines, never a single orphaned final
  character. General headings and body copy apply end protection without
  changing dates into mismatched glyph runs.
- Desktop supports asymmetric split grids, gallery fields and side indexes.
  Narrow screens collapse to one column while preserving the order of title,
  image, explanation and evidence.
- Body reading width, line height and metadata contrast take priority over
  decorative scale. At 200% zoom no essential content may be clipped.
