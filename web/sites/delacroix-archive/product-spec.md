# Delacroix Archive product specification

## Promise

Move a reader from “Delacroix is a Romantic painter” to a traceable
understanding of how his training, literature, 1832 North African journey,
state commissions, public standing and late religious murals shaped the work.

## Audiences and paths

1. A newcomer enters through the portrait and six-part biography.
2. A visual reader enters through 20 works and opens formal/context analysis.
3. A chronology reader expands any of 29 event dossiers and learns unfamiliar
   institutions and terms before interpretation.
4. A journal reader sees an exact date, current-language excerpt, editorial
   reading and edition/page locator as separate layers; French mode also pairs
   the historical wording with a modern French restatement.
5. A research reader follows exact evidence to one of 13 source records and
   uses the reverse map to return to every supported claim.

The biography explains development; chronology explains events; journal
readings show dated working life. These routes must not collapse into repeated
copies of the same list.

## Languages and state

Chinese, English and French are complete interface modes. Only the signature
and the language abbreviations remain outside the selected interface language.
Language, selected periods, saved works and notes persist locally; navigation
and language changes preserve reading position where the document permits.

First visits begin at `1798—1815` in biography and timeline, and `1822—1824`
in the journal. Returning visitors keep their most recently selected period.

The viewing experiment is a four-step learning loop: choose one question,
operate the image, record one observation, compare a conclusion, then reset.
Filters never stand alone as if they were explanations.
