# Content

## Purpose and audience

This is a Chinese-language, interactive beginner's guide to Jean-Paul Sartre's 1938 novel *La Nausée*. It helps first-time readers distinguish plot, sensory experience, later philosophical terminology, and interpretation without replacing the novel.

## Content model

- Guided reading: six short chapters that follow the novel's narrative arc.
- Story map: five Bouville locations and their narrative function.
- Nausea moments: five objects or scenes compared before and after ordinary interpretation weakens.
- Perception experiment: an interactive reading exercise, not a clinical or phenomenological simulation.
- Character files: concrete desires, losses, and narrative functions.
- Concept layers: the novel's 1938 experience layer is explicitly separated from concepts systematized in 1943–45.
- Misreading quiz: checks interpretive boundaries rather than testing quotation recall.
- Reading routes: time-based next steps and a reusable close-reading method.

## Editorial boundaries

Content must preserve four distinct levels:

1. **Historical fact** — publication history, dates, and documented biography.
2. **Scholarly interpretation** — arguments supported by the listed research.
3. **Narrative framing** — this guide's organization of plot and reading questions.
4. **Visual metaphor** — maps, evidence-board styling, and object diagrams used to support understanding.

Do not turn possibility, debate, or later theory into facts about the 1938 novel. Do not diagnose Roquentin or present the reading experiment as reproducing his experience. The site intentionally avoids long quotations and does not bind itself to one Chinese translation.
