# 两只天鹅 · Content

## Content purpose

This is a Tier B interactive visual novel about Malty and Melty. The public
landing page introduces the creator's reading of the relationship; the dialog
reader contains eleven ordered chapters and two optional memory perspectives
per chapter.

## Story boundaries

Keep these layers distinct:

- Canon reference: Malty, Melty and their original-work relationship context.
- Fan-created premise: the complete childhood, rupture, exile, rescue,
  accountability and reconciliation arc on this site.
- Story branches: each of the eleven ordered chapter records.
- Creator's critical position: accountability does not require misogynistic
  humiliation; change does not erase wrongdoing, and wrongdoing does not have
  to cancel every possible future.

Do not present invented scenes, dialogue, court decisions or future events as
canonical facts. They are part of this fan-created branch.

## Source of truth

`content-registry.js` is the authoritative production content source. It
contains:

- `chapters` — stable chapter IDs, art, summaries, dual memories and dialog
  beats;
- `acts` — the four-part landing-page reading structure;
- `storyMetadata` — the canon/fan/branch/creator boundary.

Stable chapter IDs must not change without a migration plan because saved local
progress and future deep links may depend on them.

## Editing rules

- Preserve the distinction between responsibility, punishment, reconciliation
  and forgiveness.
- Do not collapse either sister into a pure villain or pure saint.
- Preserve the creator's criticism of gendered humiliation.

- Do not add canon claims, quotations or sources without verification.
